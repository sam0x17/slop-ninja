# Retained Slop Ninja encoder Train data

This immutable snapshot publishes the remaining 2,365 Train queries from 225 works already used in the completed encoder experiment: 2,104 retained-source rows from 196 original V6 Train works and 261 rows from 29 independently acquired historical books. No new fitting, generation, detector call or Test opening occurs. Original inputs, fitted models and selection remain unchanged. These are exposed research inputs, not hidden evaluation data.

Exact text, source history, dates, author/generator identities, measured spans, missing targets and conservative exposure records are preserved. Retained-source generation used 10/40/80/100 source-span requests; the earlier book recipe used 20/60/100. Both retain separate scratch outputs, full rewrites remain mixed history at realized share one, and requested fractions are never substituted for measured final-word share. All failures/exclusions remain in audit.json; raw paid/model invocation bodies stay in ignored/private storage. Historical human proxies are not documented contemporary human-only histories.

training-membership.jsonl and training-reconstruction.json identify all 4,157 fitted Train queries from 357 works, in original fit order, across four published snapshots. Each row binds exact/normalized text hashes, source family, supervision, roles, missingness and its published row. target-contract.json retains public named-identity index meanings only; no fitted private profile vectors or weights. Metadata and targets never enter learned inputs. Full-fit source checksums remain original evidence.

Restore the lossless .zst payloads with tools/dataset-storage, then run sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS. Reproduce with publish_retained_encoder_train REPO STAGE LARGE_BATCH NEW_DATASETS_CHILD. The publisher pins both original batches and the exact fitted Train/target contract, checks ancestral rights and source bytes, validates replacement spans and reconstructs every training target before writing a new output. Parent publications remain unchanged.
