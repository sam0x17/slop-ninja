Bands, gate results and clusters come from the coordinator's score package, which the publisher verified under the trust roots and recomputed with the reward-v1 rule. No score value and no rank field is published: rows are listed in roster order, and a cluster is named by its first member in that order. The ranking is not hidden, though: the coordinator's signed row on this host (and the weights validators reveal on chain) still orders the retained clusters within a band through their credits, and gives a cluster's leftover row units to its members in rank order, until the quantization specification or a reward-rule change addresses it. Testnet results test the evaluation plumbing; they say nothing about quality on unseen text.

Security reports: security@slop.ninja (the repository's SECURITY.md describes what to send).
