# Removal record

SlopNinja removes published material only by a recorded legal removal
([terms](terms.md#legal-notices-and-removals)). This page is that record.
Each removal adds one entry when the material is taken down. Entries are
never edited or deleted: a correction, or material restored after a
counter-notice, is a new entry, and the repository's history keeps every
version of this page.

An entry holds no content of the removed material or of the notice behind it,
and no personal data. A removal changes no commitment, opening, score,
quantized result, band, gate bit, cluster or signed row, so no reward paid or
still due changes, and the removed artifact's digests stay in the public
record.

Each entry gives:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Date | the UTC date the material was taken down |
| Material | an entrant artifact's header SHA-256, its inference-content digest and its cohort; or a reference package's name and digest |
| Removed | the header, the files (each by SHA-256), or both; a file that other published material still lists, such as the allow-listed tokenizer, stays |
| Legal basis | its type and jurisdiction, for example a copyright notice under US law (17 U.S.C. § 512) or a court order |
